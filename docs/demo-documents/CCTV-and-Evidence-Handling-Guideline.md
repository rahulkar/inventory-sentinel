# CCTV and Evidence Handling Guideline

Reference LP-GDL-003 · version 1.1 · owner: Head of Loss Prevention · effective 1 May 2026

## When CCTV may be reviewed

CCTV is reviewed only after the recount and the paperwork checks for an alert (Loss Prevention and Shrinkage Policy,
section 6). Reviewing footage is proportionate when the loss is quantified and the paperwork does not explain it.

- For alerts consistent with external theft, review the sales-floor cameras covering the line's fixture for the
  flagged dates.
- For till patterns, review the till cameras only for the transactions listed in the till audit.
- For alerts consistent with internal involvement, only a loss-prevention investigator reviews footage, never the
  store team.

## Who may view footage

Footage is viewed by the store manager or a loss-prevention investigator, on site or through the CCTV system's secure
viewer. Footage is never filmed on a phone, shared on messaging apps or emailed.

## Keeping footage as evidence

- Export only the clips needed, with the camera, date and time in the file name.
- Record each export in the evidence log: who exported it, when, why, and the case reference.
- Store exports in the case system. Footage not retained for an open case is overwritten after 30 days.
- Hand footage to the police only against a written request, and log the handover (chain of custody).

## Requests from individuals

A person may ask for footage of themselves. Pass the request to the data protection officer the same day; do not
answer it in store.

## What CCTV cannot do

CCTV shows what happened in front of a camera. It does not explain a stock gap on its own: losses through receiving,
counting or keying errors never appear on camera. Close the case with the outcome the evidence supports.
